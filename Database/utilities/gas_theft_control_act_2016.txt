SECTION: 5
TITLE: Powers of the Gas Utility Court
TEXT:
(1) Subject to the provisions of this Act, a Gas Utility Court shall,—
(a) in the exercise of its civil jurisdiction have all the powers vested in a Civil Court under the Code of Civil Procedure, 1908 (Act V of 1908); and
(b) in the exercise of its criminal jurisdiction, try offences punishable under this Act and shall for this purpose have the same powers as are vested in a Court of Sessions under the Code of Criminal Procedure, 1898 (Act V of 1898).
(2) A Gas Utility Court shall not take cognizance of any offence punishable under this Act except upon a complaint made in writing by a person authorized in this behalf by a Gas Utility Company.
(3) A Gas Utility Court shall follow the procedure laid down in the CPC 1908 and CrPC 1898 where not provided for in this Act.
(4) All proceedings before a Gas Utility Court shall be deemed to be judicial proceedings within the meaning of sections 193 and 228 of the Pakistan Penal Code, 1860.
(5) No court or authority shall exercise jurisdiction with respect to any matter to which the jurisdiction of a Gas Utility Court extends, except for rights of utility companies or consumers to seek remedies before other courts/tribunals as provided.
===
SECTION: 6
TITLE: Procedure for complaints and suits for default before Gas Utility Courts
TEXT:
(1) Where a person is involved in an offence under this Act or where there are sums due or recoverable, or where a consumer has a dispute regarding billing or metering against a Gas Utility Company, a consumer or Gas Utility Company may file a complaint or suit before a Gas Utility Court as prescribed by CPC 1908 or CrPC 1898.
(2) The plaint shall be supported by a gas sales agreement, gas bill, or other documentation evidencing the contract or obligation, along with sufficient copies for defendants.
(3) The plaint in a suit for recovery by a Gas Utility Company shall specifically state the quantity of gas consumed, amounts paid with dates, and total outstanding dues up to the date of institution.
(4) Summons shall be served on the defendant through bailiff, registered post, courier, and publication in one English and one Urdu daily newspaper, which shall be deemed valid service.
===
SECTION: 7
TITLE: Leave to defend
TEXT:
(1) The defendant shall not be entitled to defend the suit unless he obtains leave from the Gas Utility Court; in default, allegations of fact shall be deemed admitted and a decree may be passed.
(2) The application for leave to defend shall be filed within twenty-one days of the date of first service.
(3) The application shall be in the form of a written statement containing a summary of substantial questions of law and fact.
(4) The application shall specifically state gas consumed, amounts paid, outstanding dues, disputed amounts, and admit/deny documents relied upon in the plaint, accompanied by supporting documents.
(7) The plaintiff shall be given an opportunity to file a reply in the form of a replication.
(8) The Gas Utility Court shall grant leave to defend if substantial questions of law or fact are raised requiring evidence.
(12) Where the application for leave to defend is rejected or conditions are not fulfilled, the Gas Utility Court shall forthwith pass judgment and decree in favour of the plaintiff.
