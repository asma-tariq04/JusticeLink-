TITLE: Consumer protection laws by province
TEXT:
Pakistan has no single national consumer law. Each province and Islamabad has its own Act: Islamabad Capital Territory: Islamabad Consumer Protection Act 1995. Khyber Pakhtunkhwa: Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Consumers Protection Act 1997 (amended in 2005, 2015 and 2017). Balochistan: Balochistan Consumer Protection Act 2003. Punjab: Punjab Consumer Protection Act 2005 (amended in 2025). Sindh: Sindh Consumer Protection Act 2014.
In Punjab, claims are filed before a Consumer Court, which is a District Judge or Additional District Judge designated by the Lahore High Court for each district. An appeal against a final order of the Consumer Court lies to the Lahore High Court within 30 days.
A user should file the claim under the law of the province where the seller or defendant lives or does business, or where the cause of action arose.
