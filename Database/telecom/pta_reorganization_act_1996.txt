SECTION: 5
TITLE: Powers of the Authority (PTA)
TEXT:
(1) The Authority shall exercise all powers as shall enable it to effectively perform its functions specified in section 4.
(2) In particular, and without prejudice to the generality of the foregoing power, the Authority shall—
(a) grant and renew licences for any telecommunication system and service on payment of specified fees;
(b) monitor and enforce licences;
(c) receive applications for radio frequency spectrum and refer to the Board;
(d) modify licences or conditions thereof;
(e) establish or modify accounting procedures for licences and regulate tariffs;
(f) regulate the transfer of licences;
(g) prescribe standards for telecommunication equipment and terminal equipment and certify compliance;
(h) provide guidelines for and determine terms of inter-connection arrangements;
(i) carry out inspections of telecommunication equipment and premises and summon any person for investigation and enquiry;
(j) appoint an Administrator where necessary;
(k) develop national telecommunication numbering plans;
(l) collect information with respect to telecommunications and review the impact thereof;
(m) enter into contracts;
(n) acquire, lease, dispose of movable or immovable property;
(o) issue regulations for exercising its powers and performance of its functions;
(p) levy fees and other charges;
(q) regulate allocation of revenues from international telephony service;
(r) undertake auctions or open competitive processes for radio frequency spectrum.

SECTION: 7A
TITLE: Establishment of the Tribunal (Telecom Appellate Tribunal)
TEXT:
(1) The Federal Government shall establish a Tribunal for the purpose of exercising jurisdiction under this Act and the Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act, 2016, consisting of a Chairperson (former Judge of High Court or qualified advocate) and two professional members (one in ICT/Telecom and one in Finance/Economics).
(4) The Tribunal shall have the same powers as are vested in a civil court under the CPC 1908 in respect of summoning attendance, requiring discovery and production of documents, receiving evidence on affidavits, and issuing commissions.
(18) The Chairperson and members of the Tribunal shall be public servants within the meaning of section 21 of the Pakistan Penal Code, 1860.
===
SECTION: 7B
TITLE: Appeal to Supreme Court
TEXT:
Any person aggrieved by an order of the Tribunal may within sixty days thereof prefer an appeal to the Supreme Court.
===
SECTION: 24
TITLE: Powers of Administrator
TEXT:
(1) An Administrator appointed under section 23 shall have powers relating to the management of the licensee, preservation of assets, remedying contraventions, and ensuring compliance with orders issued by the Authority.
(2) Upon satisfaction that the contravention has been remedied, the Authority shall withdraw the order and the Administrator shall hand over management back to the person from whom it was taken.
(3) The Authority or the Administrator shall not be liable for any loss or damages caused by any act or omission undertaken in good faith.
===
SECTION: 25
TITLE: Accounting procedures, investments and contracts
TEXT:
(1) The Authority may require any licensee to establish and maintain specified accounting procedures to obtain information for determining tariffs or exercising powers.
(2) Accounting procedures once established may only be modified after providing reasons and an opportunity of being heard.
(3) No licensee shall enter into any agreement or arrangement inconsistent with obligations under this Act, rules, or licence conditions; any such agreement shall be void to that extent.
